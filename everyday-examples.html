Liters to Gallons: Everyday Examples

Liters are common on bottles, household containers and many fuel specifications. These examples show how to estimate the equivalent volume in gallons.

Convert Your Own Amount →

Example 1: A 2-liter bottle

A 2-liter bottle contains approximately 0.5283 US gallons or 0.4399 UK Imperial gallons.

Example 2: A 5-liter container

A 5-liter container holds about 1.3209 US gallons or 1.0998 UK Imperial gallons.

Example 3: A 20-liter container

A 20-liter container is approximately 5.2834 US gallons or 4.3994 UK Imperial gallons.

Example 4: A 50-liter tank

A volume of 50 liters equals about 13.2086 US gallons or 10.9985 UK Imperial gallons.

A simple way to remember the formulas

US gallons: liters ÷ 3.7854

UK Imperial gallons: liters ÷ 4.5461

For quick estimates, 1 liter is roughly a quarter of a US gallon, or just under a quarter of a UK Imperial gallon. For exact work, use the full conversion factors.
