How to Convert Liters to Gallons

To convert liters to US gallons, multiply the number of liters by 0.264172. To convert liters to UK Imperial gallons, multiply the number of liters by 0.219969.

For example, 10 liters is approximately 2.64172 US gallons and 2.19969 UK gallons.

US Gallons vs UK Gallons

US gallons and UK Imperial gallons are different units of volume. One US gallon equals approximately 3.78541 liters, while one UK Imperial gallon equals approximately 4.54609 liters.

This difference is important when converting measurements for fuel, vehicles, cooking, manufacturing, engineering, and other applications.
