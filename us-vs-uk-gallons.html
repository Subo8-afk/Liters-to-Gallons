Unit Differences

US Gallons vs UK Imperial Gallons

The word “gallon” can mean two different volumes. Knowing which gallon is intended helps prevent mistakes when converting liters.

Use the Liters Converter →

What is the difference?

UnitExact volume in litersApproximate liters per gallon
US liquid gallon3.785411784 L3.7854 L
UK Imperial gallon4.54609 L4.5461 L

A UK Imperial gallon is larger than a US liquid gallon. Therefore, one liter equals about 0.2642 US gallons but only about 0.2200 UK Imperial gallons.

Examples using 20 liters

US liquid gallons

20 ÷ 3.785411784 ≈ 5.2834 US gallons.

UK Imperial gallons

20 ÷ 4.54609 ≈ 4.3994 UK Imperial gallons.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose US liquid gallons when the source or destination uses US customary measurements.
  • Choose UK Imperial gallons when the specification explicitly says Imperial gallons.
  • If a label simply says “gallons,” check the context rather than assuming the unit.
