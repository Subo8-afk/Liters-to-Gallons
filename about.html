About This Liters to Gallons Guide

This website provides simple educational information about converting liters to gallons, including conversion formulas, reference values and everyday examples.

Our purpose

The goal of this guide is to help visitors understand liquid-volume conversions and recognise the difference between US liquid gallons and UK Imperial gallons.

Accuracy and rounding

Conversion examples are calculated using these standard relationships:

  • 1 US liquid gallon = 3.785411784 liters.
  • 1 UK Imperial gallon = 4.54609 liters.

Tables and examples may round results for readability. If a task requires a specific precision, use the full conversion factor and follow the rounding rules relevant to that task.
