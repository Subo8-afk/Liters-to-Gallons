# Liters to Gallons Converter

A simple and lightweight converter for converting between **liters, US gallons, and UK (Imperial) gallons**.

🌐 **Try the online converter:** [Liters to Gallons](https://liters2gallons.com/)

## About

Liters and gallons are both commonly used units for measuring liquid volume, but US gallons and UK Imperial gallons are different sizes.

This project provides a simple way to convert between:

- Liters (L)
- US Gallons (US gal)
- UK / Imperial Gallons (UK gal)

You can enter a value and quickly see the equivalent measurements in the other units.

## Conversion Rates

The converter uses these standard conversion factors:

- **1 liter = 0.264172 US gallons**
- **1 US gallon = 3.78541 liters**
- **1 liter = 0.219969 UK gallons**
- **1 UK gallon = 4.54609 liters**

### Example

**10 liters** is approximately:

- 2.64172 US gallons
- 2.19969 UK gallons

## Why Are US and UK Gallons Different?

A US gallon and a UK Imperial gallon are not the same volume.

A US gallon contains approximately **3.785 liters**, while a UK Imperial gallon contains approximately **4.546 liters**.

This difference is important when converting measurements for cooking, fuel, transportation, manufacturing, science, or other applications.
